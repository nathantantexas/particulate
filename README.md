# pARTiculate Matter Art Gallery

The pARTiculate Matter Youth Art Competition, organized by Shared Skies Initiative, is collecting artworks, made by artists ages 8 to 19, under the theme *The Air We Share*. With this project, we hope to use art and creativity to spark more productive, insightful, and eye-opening conversations surrounding pollution and its impacts on human health.

**View the gallery:** https://nathantantexas.github.io/particulate/

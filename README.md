# pARTiculate Matter Art Gallery

The Shared Skies Initiative is inviting artists ages 8 to 19 to submit original work to the pARTiculate Matter Youth Art Competition under the theme “The Air We Share.” With this project, we hope to use art and creativity to spark more productive, insightful, and eye-opening conversations surrounding pollution and its impacts on human health.

**View the gallery:** https://nathantantexas.github.io/particulate/

pARTiculate Matter Art Gallery

The pARTiculate Matter Youth Art Competition, presented by the Shared Skies Initiative, invited young artists to answer one question: what do clean air and a healthy environment look like to you? Air pollution is invisible, yet it shapes the health of every community it touches, and it falls hardest on the neighborhoods least able to escape it.

The works collected here are their answers, made by artists ages 8 to 19 under the theme The Air We Share. Each one makes something unseen visible, and each imagines a cleaner and fairer future worth breathing in.
